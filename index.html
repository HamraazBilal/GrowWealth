General savings & investing guidelines

🛟

Build an emergency fund first

A common guideline is 3–6 months of essential expenses set aside before investing, so a surprise cost doesn't force you to sell investments early.

📊

Try the 50/30/20 split

Roughly 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings and investing is a widely used starting ratio — adjust it to fit your own EMI and rent load.

🌱

Consistency beats timing

Investing a fixed amount on a regular schedule (a SIP-style habit) is a well-known way to reduce the stress of guessing market highs and lows.

These are general educational guidelines, not personalized financial advice — consider speaking with a licensed financial advisor for decisions specific to your situation.
